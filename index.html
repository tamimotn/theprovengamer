HOW IT WORKS

Three stages. One path.

Start as a player, earn on results, and become a coach once you have proven you can do it consistently.

01

Qualify

Take the evaluation and win 3 ranked games in a row within 5 days.

  • Ranked solo/duo and flex games count
  • Any region
  • If the 5 days pass without it, the evaluation ends and you can try again
02

Proven

Pass the evaluation and you enter the Proven phase, where you can earn on your results.

  • A winning day is a day with more wins than losses
  • Tied days are neutral
  • After 5 winning days you can request a payout
  • A losing day ends your Proven status
03

Coach

Complete 5 payouts in the same Proven run and you graduate to coach.

  • We create and promote your coach profile
  • You set your own rates
  • Students pay through the platform and you receive the payment, minus a small commission

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which games are supported?

We are starting with League of Legends. More games may follow later.

Which games count toward my progress?

Ranked games on the account you link to your profile. Only that account is tracked.

How are my results checked?

Your results are reviewed and recorded by our team. Full details will be published before launch.

Are payouts guaranteed?

No. Payouts depend on your results and are not guaranteed. Many players will not complete every stage.

What happens if I fail the evaluation?

The evaluation ends and you can start a new one. You can also book a coach to help you prepare.
